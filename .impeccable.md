## Design Context

### Users
Startup founders, product leads, and small creative teams who need professional design work done fast without hiring. They evaluate Whenevr during research sessions, often on a laptop in a work context. They are design-literate enough to notice quality but time-poor — they want to be convinced quickly.

### Brand Personality
Confident, frictionless, premium-but-accessible. Three words: **precise, dependable, calm**.
The brand should feel like the best senior designer you've worked with — someone who just *gets it* without hand-holding. Not loud, not flashy. Understated confidence.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Light. Crisp off-white (#F0F0F0) base. This is a daytime, work-context product.
- **Typography**: Inter (display, tight tracking) paired with Source Serif 4 italic for warmth and character. The italic serif is used sparingly for emphasis — it's the brand's personality accent.
- **Color**: Near-monochromatic with a violet-purple blob as the one expressive element. Everything else stays in ink/white/gray.
- **Motion**: Confident and restrained. No bouncing, no elastic. Editorial line-reveals on the hero; staggered card entrances on scroll; smooth transitions on state changes. Motion should feel like a senior designer chose every frame.
- **Anti-reference**: Avoid agency clichés — no purple-gradient-on-dark, no neon accents, no glassmorphism.

### Design Principles
1. **Restraint over decoration** — every visual element earns its place.
2. **Typography does the work** — hierarchy through size/weight contrast, not color.
3. **Motion as editorial voice** — line-mask reveals, not fade-ins on everything.
4. **White space is intentional** — generous negative space signals premium quality.
5. **One expressive accent** — the blob gradient is the only place color goes wild; everything else is disciplined.
